Tools & experiments 6

The Lari, weeklyData dashboard

Seven years of real National Bank of Georgia rates, charted in hand-written SVG with no charting library. Indexed to a common base so five currencies two orders of magnitude apart share one honest axis — never two.
LariInvoice tool

Invoices in lari with Georgian VAT, kept in the browser with IndexedDB and printed to A4 by a stylesheet rather than a PDF library. Prints in Georgian or English without touching what you typed.
DriftfieldGenerative art

Thousands of particles drift along a Perlin flow field. The noise function and render loop are written from scratch and kept outside React state, so it holds 60fps.
SiteCheckWebsite audit tool

Paste an address, get a plain-English report on mobile layout, search visibility and credibility. Runs entirely in the browser — no backend, nothing stored.
Question TimePrint & editorial

A newspaper-style crossword generated from code — true rotational symmetry, exact numbering, print-ready PDF at two sizes.
Blindfold ChessTraining tool

Play a full game without seeing the pieces move. Legal-move validation and coordinate drilling, in a single file.

About this work. Most of these are self-initiated builds for real local businesses — concept sites I designed, built and deployed myself to work out how each type of business should be presented online. They are not paid client engagements, and the businesses shown have not commissioned them. Copy is in Georgian; I build in English just as readily.

The five React builds are a different exercise: those businesses are invented, which leaves the design free of an existing brand and lets the site be judged on what it does rather than who it is for. Each is in English, and each carries one piece of real interaction — a booking flow, a filterable list, a live quote — rather than a form that only looks like one.
